Our lab has been working hard for the past few months! We are happy to share that we now have not one, not two, but three(!!) articles published!

- The paper about alternative splicing and insecticide resistance is now available in Genome Biology and Evolution [https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evag227](https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evag227)
- The paper on timeless alternative splicing in seasonal physiology is out in Science Advances [https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aed4249](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aed4249) 
- A new paper on using High-Performance Liquid Chromatography on fly samples to detect dopamine, serotonin, and octopamine (!) is out in the Journal of Visualized Experiments [https://dx.doi.org/10.3791/74087](https://dx.doi.org/10.3791/74087)

Congratulations to Christine, Audrey, Pragya, Than, and Manny!
